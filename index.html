About

ABOUT

ilmusli designs black, tailored clothing. Each piece is made in a small run and released on its own, never in a rush.

FROM

Egypt, where ilmusli is a registered brand.

SINCE

2022. Every collection gets its own ILM code, counting up from ILM-001.

MADE

Small runs, finished by hand in Egypt. When a piece sells out, we move on to the next one instead of restocking it.

DELIVERY

We deliver across Egypt and you pay in cash when your order arrives. Delivery usually takes a few days, and you can follow every order from your profile.

CARE

Wash cold and inside out, and dry in the shade. Black fabric lasts longer when it stays away from heat and harsh detergents.

FIT

Relaxed, boxy and tailored shapes in heavy fabrics. Check the size guide on every product page, or write to us if you are between sizes.

Privacy Policy

COLLECT

Your name, email, phone numbers, delivery address, optional height, weight and sizes, and your orders and messages. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and we never see them.

USE

To create your account, prepare and deliver your orders, handle cancellations and returns, suggest a size, and answer your messages. The full details of every order are emailed to our studio inbox so we can prepare it.

SHARE

Couriers only get what they need to deliver (name, phone numbers and address). We use Google Firebase to store accounts and orders and an email service to forward order emails to us. We never sell your data.

DEVICE

Your cart and sign-in state are kept on your device so the site can remember them.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to ilmuslistudios@gmail.com.

Terms & Conditions

ORDERS

Placing an order is a request to buy. We contact you to confirm it, and may cancel it if a piece is unavailable.

PRICES

All prices are in EGP. The delivery fee depends on your governorate and grows with the distance from Cairo. You see the final total before you check out.

PAYMENT

Cash on delivery, InstaPay or e-wallet. Transfers are confirmed once we verify the payment.

DELIVERY

We deliver to every governorate in Egypt. Delivery usually takes a few days, and a little longer for far areas.

CANCEL

You can cancel an order from Profile → Orders during the first 24 hours, as long as it has not shipped. After that the option disappears.

RETURNS

Request a return from Profile → Orders within 14 days of delivery. Pieces must be unworn, unwashed and with their tags. You can follow the return request in your profile. Refunds are issued after we receive and check the piece.
